dark psychology

Signs someone uses
kindness
to control you.

#3 hurts the most
01
sign one

Their favors come
with invisible
strings.

Help you never asked for.
Gifts you can't refuse.

Later, it becomes a debt
you never agreed to.

02
sign two

They remind you
of everything
they did.

"After all I've done for you…"

Generosity that keeps score
isn't generosity.

03
sign three

Their sweetness
turns cold
when you say no.

Warm when you obey.
Distant when you don't.

That's not love.
That's training.

04
sign four

They're nicest
right before
they ask.

Compliments. Attention. Care.
Then the request.

Watch what comes
after the kindness.

what to do

Accept kindness.
Never accept
the debt.

Real kindness asks for nothing.
If it sends a bill,
it was a transaction.

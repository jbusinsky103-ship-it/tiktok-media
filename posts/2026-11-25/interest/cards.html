dark psychology

Signs your partner
is losing interest.

#4 is the hardest to notice
01
sign one

Conversations feel
like updates.

“Good.” “Busy.” “Tired.”

They’re reporting to you,
not talking to you.

02
sign two

They stopped
asking
questions.

Your day. Your plans. Your feelings.

Curiosity is the first thing
to die.

03
sign three

Plans are always
“maybe.”

Nothing is ever confirmed.
Everything can be cancelled.

You’re an option now,
not a priority.

04
sign four

They’re never
jealous
anymore.

Not trust. Not maturity.

Indifference.
They simply don’t care.

what to do

Don’t chase.
Pull back and watch
what they do.

If they want you,
they’ll close the distance.
If not — you have your answer.

dark psychology

How to spot
hidden insecurity
in anyone.

#1 is everywhere
01
sign one

They brag
constantly.

Money. Status. Who they know.

Confident people don't
need you to be impressed.

02
sign two

They can't take
a compliment.

They deflect it, joke about it,
or question it.

Deep down they don't
believe it's true.

03
sign three

They need to be
right.

Every debate is a war.
Being wrong feels like dying.

Ego that fragile
needs constant defense.

04
sign four

They overexplain
everything.

Simple question.
Five-minute justification.

They fear being judged —
so they defend in advance.

what to do

Insecure people aren't evil.
They're afraid.

Once you see the fear,
their behavior stops
controlling you.

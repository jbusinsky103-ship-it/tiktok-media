dark psychology

Signs someone is
secretly obsessed
with you.

#2 is the creepiest
01
sign one

They remember
everything.

A detail you said once,
months ago — word for word.

Nobody remembers that
by accident.

02
sign two

They know where
you’ve been.

They mention places and people
you never told them about.

They’ve been watching
quietly.

03
sign three

They mirror
your life.

Your music. Your words.
Your style, a week later.

They’re trying to
become you.

04
sign four

They react to
your silence.

You reply late and they spiral.
Double texts. Sudden coldness.

Your attention is
their oxygen.

what to do

Attention
is power.

Notice who watches you closely.
Give access slowly.
Keep some things private.

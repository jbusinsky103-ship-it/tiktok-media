dark psychology

Signs someone is
guilt-tripping
you.

#3 feels like love
01
sign one

They keep
score.

“After everything
I’ve done for you...”

Kindness with a receipt
is a transaction.

02
sign two

They sigh
instead of asking.

Silent treatment. Long faces.
“It’s fine.” It’s not fine.

You’re meant to guess
— and give in.

03
sign three

They play
the victim.

You set one boundary.
Suddenly you’re the villain.

Your “no” becomes
their pain.

04
sign four

You apologize
for nothing.

You say sorry before
you even know what for.

That reflex was
trained into you.

what to do

Guilt is a
leash.

Name it calmly:
“I won’t be guilted into this.”
Then hold your ground.
